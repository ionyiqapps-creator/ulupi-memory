which telugu movie of ravitaje released recently


Ravi Teja's latest Telugu film, Irumudi, was released on August 21, 2026. Directed by Shiva Nirvana, the action drama has garnered positive reviews, with audiences praising its emotional depth and Ravi Teja's performance. The film has also achieved strong box office collections, earning approximately ₹62.40 crore by its fourth day. However, it has faced some controversy, with the Andhra Pradesh Teachers' Federation objecting to its portrayal of government schools and teachers. Despite this, Irumudi continues to perform well in theaters, reflecting its widespread appeal.
